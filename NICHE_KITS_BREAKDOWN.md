# 🏗️ CAT-07: The 67 Niche Startup Kits Breakdown

The Mega-Vault includes **67 specialized industry vertical kits**. Each kit is a self-contained "business-in-a-box" designed to take an operator from zero to a fully operational, AI-augmented agency or startup in that specific niche.

---

## 📦 What's Inside Every Niche Kit?
Every one of the 67 kits contains **15 core vertical-specific assets**:

1.  **Vertical Prompts:** Industry-specific AI prompt libraries for marketing, sales, and ops.
2.  **Niche Templates:** Business plans, pitch decks, and Claude XML schemas for the vertical.
3.  **Execution Checklists:** 15-point startup execution matrices (licensing, equipment, etc.).
4.  **Strategic Frameworks:** Reasoning models and growth systems tailored to the niche.
5.  **Operational Systems:** Blueprints for internal niche-specific management.
6.  **Tool Stack:** Curated lists of software and hardware required for that industry.
7.  **Marketing Assets:** Campaign templates and content strategies for the niche.
8.  **Sales Scripts:** Outreach and closing frameworks for industry-specific leads.
9.  **Automation Workflows:** Importable n8n/Make.com blueprints for niche tasks.
10. **Claude Agent Prompts:** Specialized agent instructions for industry-specific roles.
11. **Analytics Dashboards:** KPI tracking and reporting templates for the vertical.
12. **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs):** Step-by-step guides for niche operations.
13. **Contract Templates:** Legal frameworks and service agreements for the industry.
14. **KPI Matrices:** Success metrics and benchmarks for that specific business model.
15. **Launch Plan:** A day-by-day roadmap for the first 30 days of operation.
